<p style="text-align: center;">
Welcome to the <b>Mechanobiology of Injury and Degeneration (MIND) Lab</b> at IIT Delhi. We are an interdisciplinary computational research laboratory residing within the <a href="https://cbme.iitd.ac.in/">Center for Biomedical Engineering</a> at IIT Delhi.
</p>

<!------>
<br>
<br>

<h2 style="text-align: right;">
  <span style="color: var(--accent-color)">WHAT</span> DO WE DO
</h2>

We study the chemo-mechanical transduction of forces in living systems, focusing on how traumatic brain injuries trigger long-term neurodegeneration. We explore how proteins such as tau respond to mechanical impacts and biochemical factors, initiating disease pathways that culminate in chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) and other neuro-degenerative disorders.

<!------>
<br>
<br>

<h2 style="text-align: right;">
  <span style="color: var(--accent-color)">HOW</span> DO WE DO IT
</h2>

Our multiscale methodologies prominently rely on physics-based computational modeling spanning:

- Continuum and molecular simulations that trace how traumatic impacts create cellular stresses and drive tau-protein phase separation and aggregation.

- Data-driven modeling that integrates high-resolution imaging and biochemical measurements from collaborators to constrain and validate our predictions.

- Mechanics-informed analysis linking molecular changes in tau and the cytoskeleton to tissue-level alterations observed after traumatic brain injury.

<!------>
<br>
<br>

<h2 style="text-align: right;">
  <span style="color:var(--accent-color)">WHY</span> IT MATTERS
</h2>

Understanding the physical triggers of neurodegeneration opens doors to early diagnostics, new personalized therapies, and pre-emptive protection of high-risk populations - from sports and military personnels to anyone facing repetitive head trauma. Our goal is to translate fundamental mechanobiological insights into practical interventions that safeguard brain health across populations.
